Robin Walters

I work on the role of symmetry in deep learning: how to build it into a model, when to relax it, and what it does to learning. I direct the Geometric Learning Lab in the Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University.

The motivating problem is data efficiency. My lab builds models that use symmetry, geometry and compositional structure to extrapolate by design rather than by scale, which matters most in robotics, science and engineering, where data is expensive and a confident wrong answer is costly.

Before Northeastern I did a PhD in mathematics at the University of Chicago with Victor Ginzburg, and spent a year at Pixar working on rendering.
